Turn 1 · Core loop
Calories Counter — crimson & navy, paper grain
One accent (crimson), navy as structural ink, a navy-tinted paper ground with inset wells. Racing shows up as language and marks — route-line charts, stage chips, a countdown to 25 Oct — not bike pictures. Style tile 1a, two alternate type tiles 1b, component sheet 1c, screens 1d.

Crimson is the only accent: ring fill, primary button, active tab, over-target, destructive. Because it carries both "good" and "danger", every state is paired with a glyph or label — never colour alone. Navy is ink and structure, not a second accent. No amber, no teal.

Surface — grain + inset wells
Page carries a faint grain. Content sits on white raised cards with a 1.5px navy-tinted border and no shadow; anything you can type into or scrub is a sunken well with an inset shadow. Two levels, no gradients.
